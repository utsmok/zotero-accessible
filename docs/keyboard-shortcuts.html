Recipes: the five things you do most

1. Save an article from the browser

  1. Make sure Zotero is open.
  2. On the article page, click the Zotero button in the browser toolbar, or press Cmd+Shift+S.
  3. The item goes into 00 Inbox and gets the red tag To read. This happens automatically. If the site offers a PDF, it is saved and renamed too.
  4. In Zotero, a large message in the middle of the list confirms the save.
  5. If Zotero was closed, the item still reaches your online library. It appears later under Unfiled Items.

2. Weekly sorting of the Inbox

  1. In Zotero, click 00 Inbox in the left pane. Also check Unfiled Items.
  2. Click an item. The right pane shows author, year, and title.
  3. To keep it: right-click the item, choose Add to Collection, and click a topic, for example 02 Topic X. It is already tagged To read.
  4. Then take it out of the Inbox: press Delete and confirm with Return. The item stays in the topic collection.
  5. If you don't need it: press Cmd+Delete and confirm with Return. It goes to the Trash.
  6. Repeat until the Inbox is empty.

3. Mark reading progress with tags

  1. Click one item. To select more, hold Cmd while clicking.
  2. Click the To read, Reading, or Read button above the list, or press 1, 2, or 3. Do the same again to remove the tag.
  3. A large message in the middle of the list confirms each change, and the whole row takes the tag's color.
  4. When you open a PDF, its item moves from To read to Reading by itself. When you finish, click Read.
  5. To see everything unread, click the saved search To read in the left pane.

4. Read a PDF comfortably

  1. Double-click the item. The PDF opens in a tab, already in Reading Mode: one clean column of text.
  2. Press Cmd++ until the text is large enough. The Aa button in the top right sets line spacing.
  3. To see the original pages, press Cmd+Shift+M. Press it again to go back to Reading Mode.
  4. To listen instead, press Cmd+Shift+L. Zotero reads aloud and marks the sentence. Press Space to pause.
  5. Press Cmd+W to close the PDF and go back to the library.

5. Cite in Word

  1. In Word, open the Zotero tab.
  2. Click Add/Edit Citation, type a few words of the title, pick the item, and press Return.
  3. At the end of the document, click Add/Edit Bibliography.
  4. One-time setup for a key instead of the button: in Word, choose Tools > Customize Keyboard, pick the category Macros, select ZoteroAddEditCitation, click in Press new keyboard shortcut, press Ctrl+Option+A, and click Assign.
