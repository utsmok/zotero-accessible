Recipes: the five things you do most

1. Save an article from the browser

  1. In Zotero, click 00 Inbox in the left pane once. New saves now land there.
  2. On the article page, click the Zotero connector button in the browser.
  3. The popup must say folder 00 Inbox. Click it. The PDF is saved and renamed automatically.
  4. If Zotero was closed, the item lands on the website account and appears later under Unfiled Items.

2. Weekly sorting of the Inbox

  1. In Zotero, click 00 Inbox. Also look at Unfiled Items (bottom of the left pane).
  2. Click an item. The right pane shows author, year, title.
  3. Keep it: drag it onto a topic collection, for example 02 Topic X. Tag it To read: press 1.
  4. Not needed: press Cmd+Delete to move it to the Trash.
  5. Done: the Inbox is empty again.

3. Mark reading progress with tags

  1. Click one item, or select several with clicks while holding Cmd.
  2. Press 1 for To read, 2 for Reading, 3 for Read. Pressing the number again removes the tag.
  3. Tagged items show their color across the whole row, not only the small dot.
  4. To see everything unread: click the saved search To read in the left pane.

4. Read a PDF comfortably

  1. Double-click the item. The PDF opens in a tab.
  2. Press Cmd+Shift+R: Reading Mode turns the PDF into one clean column of reflowed text. Press it again to go back to the original page view.
  3. In Reading Mode, use Cmd++ until the reflowed text is large enough.
  4. Tired of reading? Press L: Zotero reads aloud and marks the sentence.

5. Cite in Word

  1. In Word, open the Zotero tab.
  2. Click Add/Edit Citation, type a few words of the title, pick the item, press Enter.
  3. At the end of the document: click Add/Edit Bibliography.

Adjust any of this together before printing final copies.
